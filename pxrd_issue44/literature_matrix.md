## Audit decisions

- DOI and paper identities above are linked directly to publishers or primary preprint/review records.
- The code hashes are verified repository commit snapshots inspected on 2026-10-20. They are not claimed as the exact revisions used to produce the papers unless the primary source explicitly pins them; for these snapshots, that paper-to-commit identity was not established.
- XQueryer and SimXRD-4M are not whole-pattern inversion benchmarks; PyWPEM is physical refinement, not the same neural architecture comparison. Do not combine their metrics or call this work a reproduction of their reported performance.
