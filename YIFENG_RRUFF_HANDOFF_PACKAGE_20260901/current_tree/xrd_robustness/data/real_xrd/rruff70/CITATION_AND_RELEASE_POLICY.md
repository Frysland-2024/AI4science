# Citation and release policy

This frozen benchmark uses data from the RRUFF Project. Cite:

Lafuente, B.; Downs, R. T.; Yang, H.; Stone, N. *The power of databases: The RRUFF project.* In **Highlights in Mineralogical Crystallography** (2016), 1–29. DOI: 10.1515/9783110417104-003.

## Release boundary

- The local package contains canonical processed spectra and DIF evidence for the user's internal research workspace.
- Redistribution rights are not asserted.
- The public metadata-only package omits spectra and DIF content and retains RRUFF IDs, official record URLs, hashes, the preprocessing recipe, and the reproducibility script.
- A manuscript should cite RRUFF and identify the exact 70 RRUFF IDs in the supplementary manifest.
